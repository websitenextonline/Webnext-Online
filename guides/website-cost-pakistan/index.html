Website ka budget kin cheezon se banta hai?

Pehle website ka maqsad likhein: business introduce karna, enquiries lena, booking karwana ya products bechna. Phir zaroori pages aur features ki list banayein. Har custom feature ka design, development aur testing mein alag effort hota hai.

  1. Pages: Home, About, Services aur Contact jaise pages. Ek hi template par banne wale pages aur custom layouts ka actual quote mukhtalif ho sakta hai.
  2. Features: Ecommerce, booking, custom motion, SEO setup ya CRM integration. Sirf woh features chunen jo abhi chahiye.
  3. Content: Kya text, logo aur photos aap denge, ya unhein banana bhi project ka hissa hai?
  4. Recurring costs: Domain renewals, hosting, paid plugins, subscriptions aur maintenance ko development se alag likhein.
Example build estimate = base cost + extra pages + selected features

Is calculator mein base mein pehla page shamil hai. Har additional page alag add hota hai. Actual quotation mein design complexity, integrations, revisions aur content ki readiness bhi dekhi jati hai.

Domain, hosting aur maintenance alag rakhein

Development ek project cost ho sakti hai, jabke domain aur hosting aam tor par renewal maangte hain. Provider, plan, billing period aur renewal offer ke mutabiq rakam badalti hai. Checkout ke pehle introductory price ke saath renewal price, tax, backups aur email ki inclusion bhi check karein.

First-year budget = build + domain + hosting + paid tools + agreed maintenance

Basic SEO configuration ko Google ranking ki guarantee na samjhein. Content, competition, technical quality aur ongoing work search results par asar dalte hain. Isi tarah maintenance mein kya shamil hai, jaise updates, backup restore ya content changes, woh quote mein saaf likhwaein.

Quotation lene se pehle yeh checklist bhejein

  • Business ka maqsad, audience aur 2–3 design references.
  • Pages ki list aur har page ka expected content.
  • Booking, products, payment ya CRM ki exact requirements.
  • Text, logo aur images kaun dega, aur kab tayyar honge?
  • Domain, hosting aur paid subscriptions ka account kis ke naam par hoga?
  • Mobile checks, forms, revisions, delivery files aur maintenance ki scope.
  • Build total, recurring charges, tax aur payment milestones ki separate breakdown.

Passwords, card details ya identity documents initial enquiry mein mat bhejein. Pehle scope discuss karein aur access ki zaroorat ho to munasib secure tareeqa agree karein.
