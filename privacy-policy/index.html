Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you visit WebNextOnline, use our website tools, or contact us through this website.

Information you provide

Our enquiry form asks for your name, email address, phone number, enquiry category, selected course where applicable, and message. We use information you send us to respond to enquiries and discuss requested services, projects, or courses. Please do not enter passwords, payment-card details, identity documents, or other sensitive information into website tools or enquiry fields.

Enquiries, WhatsApp and email

The enquiry form prepares a WhatsApp message. Opening WhatsApp passes the prepared text to that service; you must press Send there to deliver it to us. Quote and contact buttons may similarly open WhatsApp or your email application with a prepared message. These services process information under their own privacy policies. Information you send may be retained in our correspondence to handle your request and any resulting work.

Design tools and browser storage

The website preview, logo maker, brand quiz, calculators, poster maker and guided Design Assistant process your entries in your browser. The Design Assistant uses pre-written guidance and does not send your questions to an external AI model.

When you use Save Design, selected design details, such as the business name, colours, layout and estimate, are stored in local storage on your device. These saved designs can remain after you close the browser until you delete them or clear website data. The enquiry form uses session storage to keep a draft during your browser session. Session restoration features may preserve that draft.

You can delete saved designs using the available tool controls or clear this website’s data in your browser settings. Exported designs and downloaded files remain wherever you save them; deleting browser data does not delete those files. Using a shared device may allow other users of that browser profile to access saved information.

Hosting and third-party resources

The website is served through GitHub Pages. Hosting and network providers may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser details and requested URLs, to deliver and protect the website.

Some pages load Google Fonts, images from Unsplash, and a Review Rover widget displaying Google reviews. Loading these resources sends technical request information to the relevant provider. Embedded third-party resources may use cookies or similar technologies under their own policies. Public reviewer names, photographs and review text may appear in the reviews widget.

Links to Google Maps, Pinterest, WhatsApp and other external websites take you to services with separate privacy practices. Their processing may take place outside your country. For more information, see GitHub’s Privacy Statement, Google’s Privacy Policy, and the privacy notices of the other services you use.

Advertising and analytics

As of the date above, the website’s own code does not include Google AdSense or Google Analytics. Search Console reports help us understand visibility in Google Search; they do not require an analytics tracking script on this website. If advertising or analytics is introduced, this policy and any required consent controls will need to be updated to describe the actual setup.

Your choices and requests

You can browse without sending an enquiry, avoid external links, and manage cookies and website storage through your browser. Blocking third-party resources or storage may affect reviews, fonts, images or saved designs. To ask about information you have sent us, request a correction, or request deletion, contact us using the details below. We may need to verify your request and retain information required for an ongoing service, a legal obligation or a dispute.

Security and policy updates

No website, device or communication service can guarantee complete security. Take care with information entered on shared devices and sent through external services. We will update this page when website data practices change and revise the date shown above.

Contact

For website privacy questions, contact WebNextOnline at websitenextonline@gmail.com.
