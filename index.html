        Frequently asked questions

        Questions? Answers.

        Key information about our services and training.

        What digital services does WebNextOnline offer?

        WordPress development, website designing, social media marketing, and graphics designing & branding. Contact us to discuss the specific scope of your project.

        Which IT courses are available?

        Python, MS Office, Artificial Intelligence, Canva, Graphics Designing, WordPress Development, and Social Media Marketing.

        How do I join a course?

        Send us a message on WhatsApp or through the contact form. We will share the latest fee, schedule, and availability before you enroll.

        How do I enroll or request a project quotation?

        Open Contact, choose Course enrollment or a service category, complete the inquiry form, and send your prepared message through WhatsApp.

        Does the inquiry form send the message automatically?

        No. It opens WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. You must press Send in WhatsApp to deliver it.
